          Zero-CA Self Filing Workflow
          
How to Upload Your GSTR-1 JSON on gst.gov.in in 4 Steps

        

        
      


      

        

          STEP 01
          
Upload E-Com CSV & Download JSON

          

            Upload your Meesho, Flipkart or Amazon GST report above, verify totals, and click Download Official GSTR-1 (.JSON).
          

        


        

          STEP 02
          
Open GST Portal Offline Tab

          

            Log in to gst.gov.in → Services → Returns → Returns Dashboard → Select Month → Click Prepare Offline under GSTR-1.
          

        


        

          STEP 03
          
Upload JSON & Verify

          

            Under the Upload tab, select your downloaded .JSON file. Within 10 seconds, the status will show Processed with 0 Errors.
          

        


        

          STEP 04
          
Generate Summary & EVC OTP

          

            Click Generate Summary at the bottom of GSTR-1, click Proceed to File, and sign with your mobile EVC (OTP)!
